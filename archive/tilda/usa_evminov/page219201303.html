Who are we?

The Yevminov's Center provides services in the field of treatment and prevention of spine diseases according to the Yevminov's Method (kinesiotherapy on an inclined plane) - a patented and approved by the Ministry of Health of Ukraine method of physical therapy for the treatment, recovery and prevention of spine diseases. Exercises for the techniques are performed on a special orthopedic simulator - Yevminov's Board (Prophylaktor).


From the moment of its opening and until today (more than 25 years!) the Yevminov's Center provides quality service in the field of treatment and prevention of spinal diseases. The basis of the provision of services is an individual approach to each client and comprehensive service for the entire family.


Scoliosis, osteochondrosis, radiculitis, intervertebral disc herniations, protrusions, scoliosis... For the treatment of most diseases of the spine, you can contact the Yevminov Center. We also offer you preventive, health, sports and other special programs.


Over the past 25 years, more than 620,000 Ukrainian families have purchased Yevminov's Board. According to the results of a survey of patients of the Yevminov Center, 90% of them recommend Board to everyone who has back problems.


Yevminov's method with the use of Yevminov's Board gives consistently high results in the recovery and treatment of spinal diseases. In most cases, it allows not to turn to other methods of treatment.


Vertebrologists are the leading specialists of the Yevminov Center. Having extensive work experience in their field, they conduct special training in the treatment and prevention of spinal diseases according to the Yevminov Method, using the Yevminov's Board.
