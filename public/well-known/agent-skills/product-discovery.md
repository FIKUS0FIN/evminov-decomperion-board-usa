# Skill: Evminov Spine Board Sizing & Ordering

1. Ask user's approximate weight (lbs), height, living setup (wall mount vs. apartment rental), and spine goal.
2. Call `POST https://evminovusa.com/api/mcp` (`calculate_traction_and_recommend_board`) or `GET https://evminovusa.com/api/ask?q=...`.
3. Present exact verified pricing ($450–$665 boards, $275–$345 stand), HSA/FSA ~30% pre-tax estimate, 60-day trial, and the returned 1-click pre-filled `orderUrl`.
